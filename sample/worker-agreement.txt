PLATFORM WORKER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. PAYMENT AND DEDUCTIONS
The Worker will receive a base payout of INR 24,000 per month. The Platform may deduct reasonable amounts for verified customer refunds, equipment damage caused by the Worker, or statutory deductions. Any deduction will be shown in the monthly payout statement. The Worker may dispute a deduction within 15 days of receiving the statement.

2. NOTICE AND RESIGNATION
The Worker may end this agreement by giving 14 days' written notice. The Platform may waive the notice period. During the notice period, the Worker must complete accepted assignments or explain any inability to do so.

3. DEACTIVATION AND TERMINATION
The Platform may suspend or terminate access at any time without assigning any reason. Immediate deactivation may occur for suspected fraud, unsafe conduct, or repeated cancellation. The Worker may request a review of a deactivation through the support portal within 7 days.

4. EXCLUSIVITY
During the term of this agreement, the Worker shall not provide substantially similar delivery services for a directly competing platform in the same city without the Platform's written permission.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising from this agreement shall be resolved by mandatory arbitration in Bengaluru. The parties agree to discuss a dispute in good faith for 15 days before starting arbitration.

6. PERSONAL INFORMATION
The Platform may process the Worker's name, contact details, location data, and delivery records to operate the service, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. The Platform will publish its privacy notice and retain information only as long as reasonably necessary.

7. GENERAL
This agreement does not describe stock options, equity grants, or ownership interests. Changes to this agreement must be in writing and accepted by both parties.
